# AudienceLens  
Understand what an audience thinks about a piece of social media content, without reading
thousands of comments by hand.

> Status: very early development. 

## The aim

Given a piece of content from a social media, AudienceLens collects the audience's comments and turns them into something you can read in a minute:

- **Sentiment classification**: is the reaction positive, negative or mixed?
- **Topics and recurring questions**: what are people actually talking about and asking?
- **Summaries**: a short, readable account of the discussion. For example: *"Viewers love the
  video, but several mention an audio problem around 7:00."*

The long-term goal is to support more platforms and more kinds of analysis. The project is
built step by step, and every step has to be measured before the next one starts.
